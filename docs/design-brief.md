Concept: "The long view." Aion is the Greek word for unbounded, enduring time. The firm's work is about places, which change over decades, and about people who'll lead them. The site should feel unhurried, precise and quietly authoritative, closer to an architecture monograph than to a consultancy brochure.
What we take from each reference
O&H Architecture: parenthetical mono labels "(Our Process)", numbered project index rows, black-and-white film-grain photography, a warm, plain-spoken voice, a live clock/"we're open" in the footer, a "Get in touch" CTA that stays in the header.
Kōzōwood: full-bleed video heroes with a scroll cue, headlines broken across staggered lines, short rhythmic copy fragments, text-link CTAs rather than heavy buttons, warm earthy palette.
Terminal Industries: numbered 01–03 pathways, confident benefit-led statements, stat-driven credibility, clear structure for a complex offer.
Fluid Glass: "Menu" toggle with a full-screen overlay, large portrait imagery at editorial scale, sentence-case headings, one persistent CTA.
Rules
Palette: warm paper, ink, stone, one accent (copper 
#B4532A, a nod to the old site's rust tile colour). Dark "ink" sections for rhythm, roughly one in every three or four.
Type: very large display serif headlines (sentence case, tight leading); clean grotesk for body; small uppercase mono for labels, numbers and metadata. At most three sizes in any one viewport.
Layout: 12-column grid, wide gutters, lots of whitespace. Asymmetric placement. Full-bleed image plates between text sections. Hairline rules instead of boxes and cards. No drop shadows, no rounded cards, no gradients, no stock icons.
Photography: real places and real rooms (cities, regeneration sites, lecture theatres, site visits). Natural light, slightly desaturated, warm. Black and white for portraits. Never generic handshake stock.
Motion: slow, precise, sparse. Line-by-line heading reveals, clip-path image reveals, gentle parallax, hover states that draw lines. 0.8–1.2s with expo-out easing. Everything respects reduced motion.
Signature details: index numbers "(01)", a live London time in the header, credentials set as text in a slow marquee, display-italic emphasis on one phrase per section.
